 📌 Abstract

QuantuMech Research Hub is an open-access STEM initiative designed to bridge the gap between rote secondary education and authentic scientific research in India. The platform integrates first-principles interactive physics and mathematics visualizers with a free, founder-led weekend video instruction portal to cultivate rigorous critical thinking and computational modeling skills among high school students.

 ✨ Key Features

 Interactive Visual Learning Lab: A suite of 10 custom-built interactive simulations allowing students to manipulate parameters and observe immediate graphical outcomes:
   Trigonometry Unit Circle: Real-time rendering of $\sin$, $\cos$, and $\tan$ wave projections.
   Pythagoras Theorem: Dynamic geometric visual proof of spatial relationships.
   Force & Motion Lab: Kinematic engine demonstrating Newton’s laws of motion.
   Solar System 3D: Orbital mechanics and gravitational simulation.
   Electricity & Circuits: Current flow and component interaction modeling.
   States of Matter: Thermodynamic particle motion visualizer (solid, liquid, gas).
   Fractions & Geometry Builders: Spatial visualizers for structural and algebraic concepts.
   Human Heart & Algebra Explorer: Biological process animation and dynamic equation plotting.
 Open-Access Video Class Portal: Integrated scheduling and enrollment framework for free live weekend workshops focusing on research methodology, mathematical problem-solving, and interdisciplinary STEM topics.
 Lightweight & High-Performance UI: Features adaptive 3D cursor parallax, customized dark/light theme toggling, and zero external engine dependencies for instant loading across under-resourced hardware.

 🛠️ Tech Stack

 Frontend Structure: HTML5 (Semantic markup, OpenGraph metadata)
 Styling & Layout: CSS3 (CSS Grid, Flexbox, Custom Properties, Media Queries for full mobile responsiveness)
 Graphics & Visualizations: HTML5 Canvas API, CSS 3D Transforms, FontAwesome Icons
 Logic & Scripting: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+, DOM Manipulation, Event Listeners, Dynamic UI Rendering1
